# v0.4.6 — Salary: gross, net, and a screen shaped like Tax

## The problem

### A. The model is sound; the ingestion is not

`salary_entry` carries `gross_minor` and `net_minor` as two independent fields,
with `bonus_minor` documented as gross-side only — a slip itemises what makes
up gross, a bank credit is one net transfer with no components. `salaryStats`
keeps the two averages apart, computes `baseTotal = grossTotal − bonus`, and
refuses to compare a gross year against a net one. That is the right model.

What reaches it is wrong. `pickAmount` (`src/lib/salary.ts`) ranks candidates by
`NET_PAY_KEYWORDS` — "k výplatě", "čistá mzda", "net pay", "take home" — and
falls back to the largest amount printed on the slip. On a normal payslip it
returns **net pay**. That one untyped number is stored in
`document.amountMinor`, and `loadSalaryHistory` (`src/lib/server/salary/history.ts`)
writes it straight into `grossMinor`.

Three consequences follow:

1. The Salary chart's gross bar is really net pay. A year evidenced by payslips
   and a year evidenced by bank credits are both net, while `avgIsGross` claims
   one of them is gross, so the guard against comparing unlike years does not
   fire when it should.
2. `baseTotal = grossTotal − bonus` subtracts a **gross** bonus line from a
   **net** total. The `grossTotal > bonus` clamp hides how far wrong that can
   go, and `baseDeltaPct` — the figure meant to answer "what did the salary
   actually do" — is drawn from it.
3. The Tax screen is affected too. `payslipYearTotalConverted`
   (`src/lib/tax.ts`) sums the same field to prefill a tax statement's **gross
   income** (`src/routes/(app)/tax/+page.server.ts:82`) and to compute the
   "payslips total X — this statement says Y" divergence note (line 134). Both
   understate gross by the tax and insurance withheld, every year, so the
   divergence note fires on every correctly-entered statement.

The interface compounds it. The upload form offers one field labelled "Amount"
with the hint "read from the slip if left blank". The payslip row renders
`45 231 CZK · bonus 8 000` with no gross/net marking anywhere on the screen.

### B. A payslip cannot be corrected or removed

- `setPayslipAmount` is a live server action (`+page.server.ts`) with **no
  caller in the UI**. The only forms on the Salary screen are `addPayslip` and
  `setBonus`. There has never been a way to fix a misread amount there.
- There is no delete on the Salary screen. `deleteDocument` exists on the
  Documents screen under the payslips shelf, far from where a person would look
  for it, and it does not touch `salary_entry` — `document_id` is
  `ON DELETE SET NULL`, so under the storage change below the figures would
  outlive the evidence.
- There is no replace. `addPayslip` always inserts a new `document` row, so
  re-uploading the correct slip for a month leaves the wrong one beside it.

### C. The bonus hint promises learning that does not happen

The screen says: "Correcting it here remembers the wording for next month."
Three defects make that untrue most of the time.

- The bonus chip is a `<button>` that swaps in an edit form, but it is styled as
  a small bordered label at `--text-xs`. It does not read as a control, which is
  why the promise looks unreachable before it even fails.
- `setBonus` chooses the slip to learn from with
  `slip.find((s) => s.storedName !== null)` over every document linked to that
  person, **with no shelf filter**. `document_link` also holds tax statements
  and contracts, so it can read a tax PDF while hunting for a payslip's bonus
  line — and even when it finds a payslip, it is not necessarily the month being
  corrected.
- `detectBonus` deliberately **sums** several bonus lines, but `learnBonusLabel`
  matches on `c.amountMinor === bonusMinor` against individual candidates. Any
  month whose bonus came from two lines can never learn, silently.

### D. The screen is shaped nothing like Tax

Salary renders a repeated per-person block: an `Eyebrow`, a chart, a payslip
list, once per person, stacked. Tax renders one screen — summary band, person
filter, chart, matrix — with the view persisted per person in `person.tax_view`.
Adding a second person to Salary therefore grows a second everything, there is
no way to see both at once, no summary, and no persisted view. There is also no
Salary or Tax entry in the floating quick-add menu, which already carries the
`?add=1` convention via `/documents?add=1`.

## Decisions taken

Confirmed with the user before writing this:

- A payslip states gross **and** net; both are read. Bonus is a component of
  gross (gross 100 000 with a 25 000 bonus means a base of 75 000).
- Net has no bonus split and never will — the slip computes net as one total.
  This is already the model's position and already how the chart draws it.
- Every payslip figure lives in `salary_entry`. `document` goes back to being
  the stored file, its month, and its person link.
- Payslips already uploaded are backfilled by **re-reading the stored PDFs**.
- Deleting a payslip **deletes the month's `salary_entry` row entirely**.
- Salary adopts Tax's layout: band → person filter → chart → table, with the
  filter in the same position Tax puts it. **Tax's band keeps its household-wide
  behaviour and its filter position; neither changes.**
- Money figures lead with **gross**, labelled, with net as a sub-line.
- "Last salary increase" is the most recent year-on-year rise in average
  monthly **base**, from the existing `baseDeltaPct`.
- The salary table is year down; base / bonus / gross / net across.
- Tax's matrix changes in two ways that Salary's table then matches: the **"All"
  summary row moves from the bottom to the top**, and the table **paginates at 5
  year-rows** with a pager centred beneath it.

### Assumption stated, not asked

The person filter sits **below** the summary band on Salary, matching Tax's
position, but Salary's band **does** answer to it — that is what "if a single
person is selected, show their figures" requires. This deliberately differs from
`TaxSummaryBand`'s stated rule that a band must not move when the filter below
it changes. Tax is left exactly as it is; only Salary's band is filter-aware.
If that reads backwards in practice, the fix is to move Salary's filter above
its band, which is a one-line change to the page and no change to any component.

## 1. Schema — one additive migration

`salary_entry` already has `gross_minor`, `net_minor`, `bonus_minor`, `source`,
`document_id`, `transaction_id`, `amount_overridden`, and the
`salary_entry_person_month_key` unique index. Nothing is added there.

One column is added: **`person.salary_view` (jsonb, nullable)**, exactly
mirroring `person.tax_view`. Nullable and additive, so the v0.3.9
additive-only lock holds.

`document.amountMinor` stays on the table — other shelves use it — but no
salary or tax code path reads it after this change.

## 2. Reader: pick both, guess neither

In `src/lib/salary.ts`:

- Add `GROSS_PAY_KEYWORDS` beside the existing `NET_PAY_KEYWORDS`:
  `hrubá mzda`, `hrubý příjem`, `hrubá měsíční mzda`, `hrubá mzda celkem`,
  `gross pay`, `gross salary`, `gross earnings`, `total gross`. Matching stays
  diacritics-insensitive via the existing `fold`, so the unaccented exporter
  variants need no separate entries.
- Add `COST_KEYWORDS` as an **exclusion**: `superhrubá mzda`, `cena práce`,
  `total cost`, `cost of employment`, `employer cost`. A candidate whose label
  matches one of these can never be selected as gross. Czech slips print total
  employment cost above gross, and it is the single most likely wrong answer.
- Replace `pickAmount` with a shared `pickBy(candidates, keywords, learnedLabel,
exclude?)` carrying the existing semantics — learned label first, then
  keywords in order, last match on a repeated label wins — and thin
  `pickGross` / `pickNet` wrappers over it.

**The largest-amount fallback is removed.** Repointed at gross it would
reliably select total employment cost; left where it is it keeps producing the
current silent mis-filing. A null gross with a form that asks once, and learns
from the answer, is the honest behaviour. `addPayslip` already has the error
path for it.

`readPayslip` returns `{ grossMinor, netMinor, bonusMinor, periodMonth,
candidates }`. Either figure may be null; the schema allows it and
`salaryStats` already reports a year over whichever months have each.

`detectBonus` is unchanged — already gross-side only, already sums multiple
lines, already deduplicates overlapping keyword hits.

## 3. Recording, correcting, learning

**Upload form**: the single "Amount" field becomes **Gross**, **Net** and
**Bonus**, all optional. At least one of gross/net is required — `recordSalary`
already enforces exactly this.

**Actions**: `setPayslipAmount` (dead, and document-scoped) is replaced by
`setPayslipFigure`, taking person, month, which field, and the amount, writing
to `salary_entry` through `recordSalary` with `overridden: true`. `setBonus`
keeps its contract, including "empty clears it back to null, which is not zero".

**Validation**, applied wherever a figure is written:

- `net ≤ gross` and `bonus ≤ gross` when both are present.
- Both are hard failures. Net above gross, and a bonus larger than the gross it
  is part of, are always data errors.

**Learned labels** split into `payslipGrossLabels` and `payslipNetLabels`;
`payslipBonusLabels` is unchanged. All three replace `payslipLabels` in the
`config-file.ts` allowlist. Existing `payslipLabels` values migrate to
`payslipNetLabels` — net is what `pickAmount` actually preferred, so that is the
truthful reading of what was learned.

**The three learning defects from problem C are fixed:**

- The slip to learn from is selected by **person and month**, joined on
  `document.shelf = 'payslips'` and `document.periodOn`. A tax statement can no
  longer be read as a payslip, and August's correction can no longer learn
  January's wording.
- `learnBonusLabel` stops requiring a single candidate to equal the stated
  total. It matches against **subsets summing to the stated amount**, preferring
  the smallest set, so a bonus assembled from two lines learns both labels. When
  no subset matches, it returns without writing — as now, but that case becomes
  rare rather than routine.
- Every editable figure gets a real affordance: a dotted underline and a pencil
  on hover/focus, `--text-sm` rather than `--text-xs`, with the hint text moved
  to sit under the table rather than promising something invisible.

## 4. Backfill

A one-off idempotent step in `src/lib/server/salary/backfill.ts`, called from
`hooks.server.ts` after `runMigrations()`, guarded by a `payslipBackfillV046`
setting so it runs exactly once per instance.

For each payslips-shelf `document` with a person link and a non-null `periodOn`:

- **Skip** if a `salary_entry` for that person-month already carries a gross or
  a net figure. Never touch a row with `amountOverridden` set.
- If `storedName` resolves and the PDF reads, write gross, net and bonus from
  the new reader, `source: 'payslip'`, `documentId` linked.
- Otherwise write the old `amountMinor` as **net**, leaving gross null. That is
  what the old reader preferred, so it is what was most likely read.

A month that lands net-only needs no marker and gets no new column: the chart
already draws it as a net tick with no gross bar and the tooltip already says
`0 gross · N net`.

The backfill logs a one-line summary — entries written, files re-read, files
missing — so a run that could not read anything is visible rather than silent.

## 5. Delete and replace

**Delete**, new on each table row. It removes the `document` row (existing
`deleteDocument`, which cascades the links and unlinks the file) **and** the
month's `salary_entry` row outright, whatever else that row held.

The confirmation names what is going — "August 2026 — gross, net and bonus" —
so a month also evidenced by a bank credit does not lose that credit's net
figure silently. The loss is recoverable: `recordSalary` writes a bank credit's
net when a transaction is filed as salary
(`src/lib/server/transactions/index.ts`), so re-filing that transaction rebuilds
the month.

**Replace** is not a separate operation. `salary_entry` is uniquely keyed on
person-month, so uploading a slip for a month that already has one updates that
month's figures in place rather than inserting a second row. `recordSalary`'s
existing rule still applies and is wanted here: a figure with
`amount_overridden` set is not overwritten by an automatic reading, so a
correction survives a re-upload. A figure typed into the upload form counts as
overriding and does replace it.

When the existing month already has a stored file, `addPayslip` replaces it:
the new document row is written and the old one deleted through
`deleteDocument`, which unlinks the old file after its row is gone. The two
document writes are not one transaction — `deleteDocument` deliberately unlinks
the file outside one — so the order is new-then-old, and a failure between them
leaves two documents for the month rather than none.

## 6. Downstream data

- `loadSalaryHistory` reads every figure from `salary_entry` and uses `document`
  only for the file list and the person link. The two-source merge that caused
  this defect is deleted, not repaired.
- `payslipYearTotal` / `payslipYearTotalConverted` in `src/lib/tax.ts` become
  `salaryYearGrossTotal` / `salaryYearGrossTotalConverted` over salary entries,
  summing `grossMinor` and counting only months that have one. The tax prefill
  and the divergence note then use real gross, and the note stays silent for a
  year with no gross months rather than comparing a statement against nothing.
- `src/routes/(app)/tax/+page.server.ts` is updated at both call sites.

## 7. The Salary screen, rebuilt

Layout, top to bottom, mirroring Tax:

```
ScreenHeader "Salary"          — actions snippet: "Add payslip"
SalarySummaryBand              — answers to the filter (see Assumption above)
Segmented person filter        — Both · <person> · <person>, when >1 person
SalaryYearChart                — both series when "Both" is selected
SalaryMatrix                   — "All" row on top, 5 year-rows, pager
```

The repeated per-person block is removed. `data.history` still arrives per
person; the page selects from it rather than iterating it into sections.

### Summary band

Gross is the headline in every figure, with net beneath it as a sub-line, and a
dash where a year has no gross. Figures differ by filter, as agreed:

**Both selected** — three figures, household totals across the selected people:

| Figure                    | Value                                         |
| ------------------------- | --------------------------------------------- |
| Earned since {first year} | Σ gross, all years · sub: Σ net               |
| Average year              | Σ gross ÷ years on record · sub: same for net |
| Last year                 | most recent year's Σ gross · sub: its Σ net   |

**One person selected** — four figures:

| Figure                    | Value                                                                                                                                                     |
| ------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Earned since {first year} | Σ gross · sub: Σ net                                                                                                                                      |
| Average month             | Σ gross ÷ gross months, lifetime · sub: same for net                                                                                                      |
| Last increase             | most recent year with `baseDeltaPct > 0`, as "+8.2% · 2025" · sub: "base pay, bonus excluded". A record with no rise shows "—" and "no increase recorded" |
| Average month, last year  | most recent year's gross ÷ its gross months · sub: same for net                                                                                           |

The band uses the existing `auto-fit, minmax(190px, 1fr)` grid, so three and
four figures both lay out without a special case.

### Chart with two people

`SalaryYearChart` gains an optional second series. Colours come from a new
`src/lib/series-hues.ts` holding `seriesHues(keys: string[])`, which assigns
palette tokens by array order. `src/lib/tax-hues.ts` keeps `countryName` and
redefines `hueTokens` as `seriesHues([...new Set(countries)].sort())` — Tax's
colours are byte-identical afterwards. People are passed in creation order
(`person.createdAt, person.id`, the order `loadSalaryHistory` already uses), so
a person's colour never changes when another is added. The legend names the
people; the existing gross/base/bonus/net key stays.

### Salary table

`SalaryMatrix`, styled on `TaxMatrix` — same grid-per-row construction, same
right-aligned numerics, same expand-a-row-for-detail gesture, same magnitude bar
confined to the one all-same-currency column.

Columns: `Year · Base · Bonus · Gross · Net · Change`. `Change` is
`baseDeltaPct`, so a one-off bonus never reads as a raise. Expanding a year
reveals its months: month, gross, net, bonus, the linked payslip file, and the
per-figure edit and delete controls.

### Quick-add

Two entries are added to `quickAdds` in `src/routes/(app)/+layout.svelte`, gated
on their modules like every existing entry:

- `{ href: '/salary?add=1', label: 'Payslip', icon: 'wallet' }`
- `{ href: '/tax?add=1', label: 'Tax statement', icon: 'receipt' }`

Both screens read `add=1` on load and open their form or dialog — `/documents`
already establishes this convention. The param is stripped from the URL after
opening, via `replaceState`, so a refresh does not reopen the form.

### Persisted view

`person.salary_view` stores `{ person, mode, page }` through a
`/salary/prefs` `PUT` endpoint and a `normaliseSalaryView` validator, both
copied in shape from `/tax/prefs` and `normaliseTaxView`, including its
last-write-wins rationale and its rule that unknown values fall back rather
than throwing.

## 8. Tax table changes

Two changes to `TaxMatrix`, which `SalaryMatrix` then matches:

- **The "All" row moves from the bottom to the top**, between the header and the
  year rows. It is lifetime and does not change when the table pages.
- **Pagination at 5 year-rows.** A pager sits centred beneath the table showing
  "‹ 1 / 3 ›" with the year range on each page. The magnitude bar's `ceiling`
  stays computed over **all** years, not the visible page, so bar lengths remain
  comparable as you page. An expanded year collapses when its page leaves view.
  Below 6 years the pager is not rendered at all.

The page index is persisted alongside the other view prefs on both screens.

Nothing else about Tax changes — not the band's household-wide behaviour, not
the filter's position, not the chart.

## 9. Testing

Test-driven, per the usual workflow.

**Unit** (`tests/unit/salary*.test.ts`):

- gross picked by keyword; net picked by keyword; both picked from one slip
- gross-only and net-only slips each yield null for the other field
- a `superhrubá mzda` line is never picked as gross, even when it is the largest
  amount and no gross keyword matched
- no largest-amount fallback: a slip with no pay keyword yields null, not a guess
- learned gross and net labels do not overwrite each other
- `learnBonusLabel` learns both labels from a two-line bonus summing to the
  stated total, and writes nothing when no subset matches
- `net > gross` and `bonus > gross` both rejected
- summary-band figures for the household case and the single-person case,
  including "no increase recorded" when no year rose
- `seriesHues` assigns by order; `hueTokens` produces the same map for the same
  countries as before the extraction

**Integration** (`tests/integration/salary-entries.test.ts`, plus new
`salary-backfill.test.ts`):

- backfill writes an entry from a legacy document with a readable file
- backfill falls back to net when the file is missing
- backfill is idempotent on a second run
- backfill leaves an `amountOverridden` row untouched
- delete removes both the document and the month's entry
- re-uploading a slip for an existing month updates in place and replaces the
  stored file rather than creating a second document
- a corrected (`amount_overridden`) figure survives a re-upload's automatic
  reading, but is replaced by a figure typed into the upload form
- `setBonus` learning ignores a tax-shelf document linked to the same person
- `/salary/prefs` rejects an unknown person id and falls back rather than throwing

**Tax**: prefill reads gross; the divergence note is silent for a year with no
gross months; the matrix renders the "All" row above the year rows; the pager
appears above 5 years and not at or below it; the magnitude bar's ceiling is
unchanged by paging.
