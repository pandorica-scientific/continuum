# Continuum Corporate Contributor Licence Agreement

**Version 1.0**

This is the form for an organisation whose employees contribute to Continuum in
the course of their work. It exists because in most jurisdictions an employer,
not the employee, owns what the employee writes on the job — so an individual
agreement signed by that employee may not be worth anything.

If You are contributing on Your own time and Your employer has no claim on the
work, sign the individual agreement (`CLA.md`) instead. You do not need both.

It is adapted from the Apache Software Foundation's Corporate Contributor
License Agreement v2.0.

---
