# Continuum Individual Contributor Licence Agreement

**Version 1.0**

Thank you for contributing to Continuum. This agreement records the terms under
which your contribution is accepted. It exists so the project can be maintained,
relicensed and distributed over its whole life without having to find every past
contributor for permission each time.

It is adapted from the Apache Software Foundation's Individual Contributor
License Agreement v2.0, which is the common form for this and has been read by
far more lawyers than a bespoke one would be.

## What this does, in plain terms

**You keep the copyright in what you write.** This is a licence, not an
assignment. You may use your own contribution anywhere else, under any terms,
for ever.

**You give Robert Kiewisz permission to license your contribution to others,
including under licences that are not the AGPL.** That is the operative part.
Continuum is published under the GNU AGPL v3 and the intent is that it stays
that way. But the project is also the basis of commercial work — native
applications, and paid licences for organisations that cannot accept copyleft
terms — and without this permission a single outside patch would make that
impossible without tracking down its author.

**You are not promising your contribution is perfect**, only that it is yours to
give.

If you are not comfortable with the second point, please say so rather than
signing. An unsigned patch can still be discussed, and a bug report or a
reproduction costs you nothing to file.

## Signing

Contributions are checked automatically. When You open Your first pull request,
a bot will ask You to signal Your agreement by leaving a comment on it; that
record is kept against Your account and later pull requests pass without asking
again.

If You would rather sign out of band, email the maintainer with the text below
and the pull request will be marked accordingly.

and I agree to it.
