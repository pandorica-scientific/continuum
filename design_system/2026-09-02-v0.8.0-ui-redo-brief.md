# Continuum v0.8.0 — UI redo brief for Claude Design

> **Status.** The UX of v0.8.0 is approved (2 September 2026) and is fixed. The
> visual layer is open: this brief asks for a fresh look on top of the approved
> structure, delivered as a design system plus every screen. The reference
> canvas of the eight Documents shelves in the approved frame is at
> <https://claude.ai/code/artifact/af3597d3-f953-4255-98a5-50d1a532464c>.
>
> Attach with this brief: `docs/ui-guidelines.md` (the rules the code enforces),
> `src/lib/styles/app.css` (today's tokens), `design_system/README.md` (the v4
> handoff, for rationale), and `docs/screenshots/` (every screen as it ships
> today, both themes, some mobile).

## 1 · What Continuum is

A self-hosted web app for one household: bank accounts across several banks and
currencies, imported statements, two flats, investments, loans, a retirement
projection, a calendar that syncs both ways, Home Assistant devices, and a
document archive. Two adults use it, on a laptop mostly and a phone sometimes.
Everything visible is configuration: people, currencies, accounts, properties
and whole modules are data, and switching a module off removes it from the
sidebar.

It ships as a Docker image people run themselves. Nothing may call out to a
CDN, a font host or an icon service at runtime. Fonts are self-hosted files.

## 2 · What is fixed: the approved UX

Do not redesign any of this. Restyle it.

### 2.1 The screen frame, on every screen

1. **Screen header**: title with a mark, a one-line caption, optional sub-tab
   row when the area has more than one screen. On a Documents shelf the title
   is the shelf and the caption is the shelf's question.
2. **Summary band**: a row of three metric tiles. Label, figure, note. A figure
   takes a colour only when it is a task (amber for watch, red for bad).
3. **Control row**: search on the left, secondary actions, one primary action
   on the right. One primary action per screen, never two.
4. **The content**: the screen's own layout.
5. **Sidebar**: seven areas (Overview, Money, Assets, Retirement, Home,
   Calendar & Contacts, Documents), net worth above them, theme toggle and the
   signed-in household at the foot. Each area carries one hue on its icon.

### 2.2 The Documents redesign

A **shelf is one question, one unit, one template**. Eight ship:

| Shelf | Question | Unit | Template |
|---|---|---|---|
| Inbox | What still needs deciding? | document | queue |
| IDs | Does everybody hold a valid document? | person | wallet |
| Statements | Is any month missing? | account | completeness (ribbon) |
| Income & Tax | Which filing never arrived? | organisation | dossier |
| Health | What happened to this person, and when? | person | dossier (timeline preset) |
| Inventory | Is this still under warranty? | item | dossier (kit preset) |
| Property | What does this address require of us? | property | dossier |
| Vehicles | Is this vehicle covered and legal? | vehicle | dossier (obligations preset) |

Seven template names are offered when a household makes its own shelf; four
engines draw them.

**Queue** (Inbox): one document at a time. Preview left, decision right in
three steps: shelf, card on that shelf, lane on that card. A rule proposal
pre-fills all three. File, Skip, Delete.

**Wallet** (IDs): one card per document at credit-card proportion, sectioned by
person with the person's colour tag, country badge top right, kind and expiry
pill bottom left. Documents nobody is named on gather under "Nobody" at the end.

**Ribbon** (Statements): one row per account, twelve months across, one year at
a time. A filed statement is a solid band spanning the months it covers. A
missing month is a red outline, always one month wide. A month not yet due is
dashed. Months before the account existed are faint. A second block draws
yearly reports per decade.

**Dossier** (the other five): one card per unit, sorted so cards with a finding
come first, expanded by default, collapsible to one line by their header.
Inside a card, top to bottom:

- header: emoji, name, kind chip, document count, a three-dot menu;
- a relationship line where there is one (role and since; let to whom);
- the pinned document the relationship rests on;
- lanes, each a label, a cadence and a row of cells (filled, missing, not due
  yet, before the relationship began), with a filed-of-expected count;
- expected slots, a lane with cadence once: receipt, warranty, manual, drawn
  as one labelled cell each, filled or missing;
- history, for paper with no rhythm, newest first.

A "New card" affordance ends the stack. An implicit last card, "Not assigned
yet", holds paper on the shelf that names no unit and draws only when it has
something in it.

**Rail**: Inbox, the shelves (rename and reorder behind a pencil), Everything.
Nothing else. Subjects and organisations are cards on their shelves, not rail
rows.

**List mode**: every shelf has an "as a list" link. Title, tiles and control
row stay; the layout area becomes a control strip (group, sort, select, type,
about and tag filters) and a grouped table. Bulk edit lives here only. A search
always lands here. Everything is this list with no shelf.

**Inspector**: a right-hand panel opened by any row or card. Header with the
title and a lock when restricted, preview, actions, then the sections: about
(chips), dates, identity fields for an ID document, extraction details for an
admin. Read and edit share one layout; Save and Cancel replace the Edit control
where it was, never at the foot of a long form.

### 2.3 Semantics that must survive any restyle

- **Green, yellow and red always mean state**: good, watch, bad. Never
  decoration. **Blue, teal and purple carry a data series.** Grey is neutral.
- **Every number is set in the figures face**, tabular. Balances, dates in
  lists, counts, tickers, cell labels.
- **Emoji survive only where the household chose them**: shelves, accounts,
  subjects, and as a screen-title prefix. Never as section markers.
- **Icons are one stroke set**: 24 grid, no fill, `currentColor`, 1.7 stroke,
  round caps. Redraw them if the new look wants a different weight, but keep
  one geometry across the set.
- **Dark and light are both first-class.** Today dark is the default and light
  an opt-in. The redo may flip that. Both must pass WCAG AA on every pill and
  tile, and a pill that fails is fixed by darkening its ink, not by re-mixing
  its tint from that ink.
- **Coloured borders only on traffic-light pills.** Nothing in the document
  flow is raised; a shadow belongs only to what floats (menus, pickers,
  tooltips), and floating surfaces are painted opaque.
- **One control height** for every input, select and button in a row.
- **Wide content scrolls inside its own container.** The body never scrolls
  sideways. A phone gets the same screens, one column, with the sidebar as a
  drawer.

## 3 · What is open: the redo

Everything visual. In particular:

- **Colour.** The ground, the card treatment, the seven area hues, the accent.
  A palette that is calm at rest and lets the three state colours be the only
  loud thing on a screen.
- **Type.** A display face for screen titles and card names, a body face, and
  the figures face. Keep a ramp of about nine steps; the code snaps every size
  to a step. Sizes and weights per role are yours to set.
- **Shape and surface.** Radii, whether cards are bordered or tonal, spacing
  scale, the density of a list row, the height of a tile.
- **The card language**, since v0.8.0 makes the card the unit of the archive:
  the metric tile, the dossier card and its lanes and cells, the wallet card,
  the ribbon cell, the list row, the rail row, the inspector panel.
- **Iconography**: keep one stroke set, redraw freely; add a coloured badge
  treatment if the direction wants it.
- **Empty states, dialogs, the review queue, the mobile drawer.**
- **The 404 and error pages** (`design_system/Continuum Error Pages.dc.html`
  holds the current ones).

## 4 · Direction

- **Light and airy**: white cards on a warm off-white ground, wide gutters,
  generous 16px-class radii, nothing raised, hairline dividers inside cards.
- **A coloured icon badge per module**: a small filled disc holding a white
  glyph, one hue per module, repeated in the sidebar and on the card header.
  This is how each area gets an identity without a gradient.
- **Initials avatars in a person's hue**, used consistently for people.
- **A big, warm screen title** ("Good afternoon, Linda") with a small eyebrow
  above it (the date), and an "at a glance" grouped list under it.
- **Capsule controls** and small rounded count chips.
- **Calm type**: a system-quality sans, few sizes, weights 400 and 600, no
  letter-spaced uppercase except eyebrows.
- **Both themes verified for contrast.** Their dark theme is a near-black
  with the same card language, not an inverted light theme.

What we keep from Continuum v0.7.x: figures in a mono face,
the traffic-light meaning of green, yellow and red, dense readable tables, and
the refusal to decorate. Do not bring in gradient washes, glass blur, drop
shadows on in-flow cards, more than one accent, or emoji as icons.

## 5 · Where the design system stands today

Use these as the starting point, not as a constraint. Every value comes from
`src/lib/styles/app.css`.

**Dark ground and ink**: bg `#0e1117` to `#11161f`, sidebar `#0a0d13`, cards as
white at 3, 6 and 9 percent, borders white at 8 and 18 percent, ink `#e6e9ef`,
`#c5ccd6`, `#99a4b3`.

**Light ground and ink**: bg `#f3f0e9` to `#eeeae1`, sidebar `#efebe2`, cards
`#ffffff`, `#f7f4ee`, `#ebe6dc`, borders `rgba(60,52,40,0.1)` and `0.2`, ink
`#1c1a16`, `#494339`, `#6b6559`.

**Hues** (dark / light): green `#2ecc71` / `#0e7a4a`, yellow `#f1c40f` /
`#8a5900`, red `#ef6a5c` / `#bd2e21`, blue `#5aaee4` / `#186294`, purple
`#bd85d3` / `#743990`, orange `#e67e22` / `#a2530f`, teal `#1abc9c` / `#087059`,
indigo `#7b8ce8` / `#454fb0`, brand `#4a86c8` / `#1b4f8a`. Each hue has a tint
(pill fill, alpha 0.18 dark, 0.13 to 0.16 light) and a wash (card ground, about
0.07). Light tints are mixed from bright hues, never from the dark ink.

**Area hues**: Overview brand, Money teal, Assets purple, Retirement blue,
Home orange, Calendar & Contacts indigo, Documents neutral.

**Type**: Inter variable for text, Source Code Pro for figures, both
self-hosted. Ramp 10, 11, 12, 13, 14, 16, 19, 22, 28. Screen title 28 at 600
with tight tracking, caption 13, section heading 22, eyebrow 11 uppercase
spaced, metric value 19 mono 600, body row 13, sidebar item 13.

**Geometry**: radii 4, 6, 8, 10, 12, 16, pill. Space scale 2, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12,
14, 16. Control height 36. Sidebar 252. Main padding 26 32 60, section gap 26.
Metric tile padding 12 14, content card 16 18. Borders 1px unless width means
something. Two shadows exist, float and raise, and only floating elements use
them.

**Primitives in code** (`src/lib/components/`): `ScreenHeader`, `MetricTile`,
`Pill`, `Icon`, `Sidebar`, `UploadDropzone`, `ListPager`, `EmojiPicker`,
`TagsPanel`, plus `.btn`, `.btn-primary`, `.icon-btn`, `.pick-chip`, `.chips`,

`ShelfBanner` (being removed), `WalletView`, `CoverageView`,
`CounterpartiesView`, `DocumentsRail`.

## 8 · Acceptance

A screen is done when it passes the checklist in `docs/ui-guidelines.md`:

- no colour literal outside the token sheet;
- every figure in the figures face, tabular;
- checked in both themes, AA contrast on every pill and tile;
- borders 1px unless the width carries meaning; no shadow in the flow;
- pill hue means state, never decoration;
- one primary action per screen;
- keyboard focus visible; reduced motion respected;
- wide content scrolls in its own container;
- the same frame, in the same order, on every screen.
