Emoji picker · recommendation
A 6×4 grid of 36px cells — one --control-h square each, so the grid is 236px wide and needs no scroll — plus a two-character text field for anything else. Justification: external pickers are banned and a self-hosted app on Linux cannot rely on an OS picker being reachable. Twenty-four covers the household domain; the field covers the twenty-fifth without shipping an emoji database.
Open call · emoji in the main rail Recommendation: settings list and screen title only — not the rail. The rule allows emoji at row level, and rail entries are rows, so this is a judgement call rather than a rule call. Three reasons to keep them out: the app sidebar directly to the left renders 19px <Icon> glyphs, and a second glyph column 252px away would read as two competing icon systems; the 218px rail already carries a right-aligned mono count, and a 20px emoji gutter costs the label its last two characters at the widths where shelf names are longest; and the rail's job is switching, where the active fill does the work a glyph would. The emoji still appears where it is chosen (settings) and where it identifies rather than navigates (the 🚗 Car screen title), which is the sanctioned prefix position — so nobody loses sight of their choice.
